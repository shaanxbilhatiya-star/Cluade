## 🔴 नई धाराएं जोड़ी गईं (NEW CLAUSES ADDED)

### 1. सर्वोत्तम प्रयास (Best Efforts) — धारा 1.2

**Hindi:** Ruralift की सेवाएं "सर्वोत्तम प्रयास" आधारित हैं — कोई गारंटी नहीं।

**English:** Added explicit "best efforts" language. This is the single most important protective clause. If a client ever sues claiming "you promised my loan would be approved," this clause makes clear Ruralift NEVER guaranteed any outcome. Without this, a court could interpret silence as an implied guarantee.

**Legal Basis:** Indian Contract Act §73 — damages only for foreseeable loss. Best efforts caps expectations.

---

### 2. मुख्य-से-मुख्य संबंध (Principal-to-Principal) — धारा 1.3

**Hindi:** Ruralift ग्राहक का न्यासी, प्रत्ययी या एजेंट नहीं है।

**English:** Explicitly states that Ruralift is NOT a fiduciary, trustee, or agent of the client. This is critical because fiduciary relationships create much higher legal duties (duty of loyalty, duty of care, duty to disclose). By clearly establishing a principal-to-principal relationship, Ruralift avoids these elevated duties. A client cannot claim "you had a fiduciary duty to get me the best rate."

**Legal Basis:** Indian Contract Act §182-238 (Agency law) — explicitly opting OUT of agency relationship.

---

### 3. गैर-परिवर्जन (Non-Circumvention) — धारा 5

**Hindi:** ग्राहक 12 महीने तक Ruralift द्वारा परिचित कराए गए किसी भी संपर्क से सीधे सेवा नहीं लेगा।

**English:** NEW dedicated section preventing the client from bypassing Ruralift to directly approach lenders/contacts introduced by Ruralift — for 12 months after contract ends. This protects Ruralift's business relationships and prevents clients from using Ruralift's introductions without paying fees. The original only had a weak "don't hire another agent during the contract" clause.

**Legal Basis:** Enforceable as reasonable restraint on trade under Indian Contract Act §27 (reasonable restrictions upheld by courts).

---

### 4. दायित्व की सीमा (Limitation of Liability) — धारा 6

**Hindi:** Ruralift का अधिकतम दायित्व = ग्राहक द्वारा भुगतान की गई राशि तक। परिणामी हानि पूर्णतः बहिष्कृत।

**English:** CAPS Ruralift's maximum liability at the fee actually paid (or ZERO if no fee paid). Also EXCLUDES all consequential, indirect, incidental, and special damages. This means:
- Client cannot sue for "lost business opportunity" because loan was delayed
- Client cannot claim "mental anguish" damages
- Client cannot claim "loss of reputation"
- Maximum payout = fee received (likely ₹0 in most dispute scenarios)

**Legal Basis:** Valid under Indian Contract Act §73-74. Indian courts uphold liability caps in commercial contracts.

---

### 5. क्षतिपूर्ति (Indemnification) — धारा 7

**Hindi:** ग्राहक Ruralift को किसी भी दावे, हानि, लागत से क्षतिपूर्ति करेगा जो ग्राहक के कार्यों से उत्पन्न हो।

**English:** If a client provides fake documents and the lender sues Ruralift, or if a third party brings claims against Ruralift because of the client's actions — the CLIENT must pay all costs including lawyer fees. This is a shield against cascading liability.

**Legal Basis:** Indian Contract Act §124-125 (Contract of Indemnity).

---

### 6. अप्रत्याशित घटना (Force Majeure) — धारा 12

**Hindi:** Ruralift के नियंत्रण से परे घटनाओं (महामारी, बैंक हड़ताल, RBI नीति परिवर्तन आदि) के लिए कोई दायित्व नहीं।

**English:** Protects Ruralift if external events prevent service delivery — bank policy changes, RBI regulatory changes, pandemics, banking strikes, system failures. Without this, client could argue "you failed to deliver, so refund/compensate me." Now Ruralift is explicitly protected from events outside its control.

**Legal Basis:** Indian Contract Act §56 (Doctrine of Frustration) + contractual force majeure (upheld by Supreme Court in Energy Watchdog v. CERC).

---

### 7. संचार का माध्यम (Communication Validity) — धारा 13

**Hindi:** केवल लिखित संचार (WhatsApp, SMS, ईमेल, पत्र) मान्य। मौखिक बातचीत अमान्य।

**English:** CRITICAL protective clause. Prevents clients from later claiming "your agent verbally promised me X." Only written communications (WhatsApp, SMS, email, physical letter) are valid. Oral conversations cannot modify the contract. This eliminates the "he said, she said" problem that is very common in loan facilitation disputes in rural India.

**Wordplay/Strategy:** The Hindi uses "अमान्य" (invalid) — very strong language that a court will interpret literally.

---

### 8. मध्यस्थता (Arbitration) — धारा 14.3

**Hindi:** विवाद मध्यस्थता से सुलझाया जाएगा। मध्यस्थ Ruralift द्वारा नियुक्त। स्थान = Ruralift का जिला।

**English:** Replaced simple "go to court" with mandatory arbitration. Key advantages for Ruralift:
- **Ruralift appoints the sole arbitrator** (massive strategic advantage)
- **Venue = Ruralift's district** (client must travel, not Ruralift)
- **Faster and cheaper** than civil court litigation
- **Private** — no public record that could damage reputation
- **Final and binding** — limited appeal options for client

**Legal Basis:** Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Fully enforceable.

## 🟡 मौजूदा धाराओं में किए गए परिवर्तन (CHANGES TO EXISTING CLAUSES)

### 11. PDC Auto-Extension: 7 दिन → 30 दिन (बार-बार)

**पहले:** चेक दिनांक 7 दिन आगे बढ़ेगी (एक बार)।
**अब:** 30 दिन आगे बढ़ेगी, और यह **बार-बार** (repeatedly) लागू होगा जब तक Ruralift जमा/वापस न करे।

**Why:** 7 days was too short — if Ruralift misses the window by even a week, the PDC becomes stale and unenforceable. 30-day rolling extension gives Ruralift unlimited time flexibility and prevents the cheque from ever going "stale dated" (banks reject cheques older than 3 months from date).

---

### 12. शुल्क अपरिवर्तनीय (Fee Irrevocability) — धारा 2.3

**पहले:** कोई प्रावधान नहीं था।
**अब:** एक बार ऋण वितरित = शुल्क बिना शर्त देय — भले ही ग्राहक बाद में ऋण रद्द करे।

**Why:** Some clients might cancel their loan after disbursement and then argue "loan didn't happen, so no fee." This clause blocks that argument entirely.

---

### 13. विलंब शुल्क (Late Payment Penalty) — धारा 2.4

**पहले:** कोई प्रावधान नहीं था।
**अब:** 7 दिन में भुगतान न होने पर 2% प्रति माह (24% वार्षिक) विलंब शुल्क।

**Why:** Creates urgency. Without penalty, clients delay payments indefinitely. 24% annual is within legally permissible limits for service contracts.

---

### 14. अनुबंध भंग — विस्तारित परिभाषा (Expanded Breach Definition) — धारा 4.1

**पहले:** 5 प्रकार के भंग।
**अब:** 7 प्रकार — जोड़े गए:
- (फ) समानांतर एजेंट नियुक्त करना (बिना सूचना)
- (ग) **मानहानि/अपमान** (defamation clause)

**Why:** Anti-defamation clause is CRITICAL. In rural loan facilitation, unhappy clients often badmouth agents on WhatsApp groups or social media. Now this constitutes a "breach" with financial consequences. Very strong deterrent.

---

### 15. नुकसान भरपाई — "जो भी अधिक हो" (Liquidated Damages — Whichever is HIGHER)

**पहले:** सहमत ऋण राशि का 3%।
**अब:** **ऋण राशि का 3% या सहमत परामर्श शुल्क — जो भी अधिक हो।**

**Why:** For small loans, 3% might be less than the consultation fee. This ensures Ruralift always recovers at least the full fee amount as minimum damages.

---

### 16. धारा 138 NI Act — स्पष्ट चेतावनी (Explicit Criminal Warning)

**पहले:** "दायी होगा" — vague reference.
**अब:** "दो वर्ष तक का कारावास और/या चेक राशि के दोगुने तक का जुर्माना" — **specific consequences stated.**

**Why:** When a client reads "2 years jail possible," it creates real psychological deterrent. Vague legal references have less impact on rural/semi-urban clients.

---

### 17. ग्राहक घोषणा — नए बिंदु (Strengthened Client Declaration)

**जोड़े गए बिंदु:**
- "मुझे स्वतंत्र कानूनी सलाह लेने का अवसर दिया गया" — यह भविष्य में "मुझे धोखा दिया गया" दावे को रोकता है
- "गलत जानकारी देने पर मैं स्वयं उत्तरदायी" — self-declaration of liability
- "Ruralift का कोई नियंत्रण नहीं" — reinforces no-guarantee position
- डेटा साझा करने की सहमति — prevents privacy complaints later

**English:** These additions create an airtight "informed consent" record. If client ever goes to consumer court saying "I was misled," Ruralift produces this signed declaration showing client had full knowledge and opportunity for independent legal advice.
